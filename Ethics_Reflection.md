
## 1. Why is it important to verify data collected from public APIs?

because the data may contain errors, missing values, duplicates, or outdated information. Checking and validating the data helps ensure that the analysis is accurate and reliable.

## 2. Why should data analysts document the source of their data?

Data analysts should document the source of their data to make the analysis transparent and trustworthy. Recording the API or dataset source helps others understand where the data came from and makes it easier to verify, reproduce, or update the analysis.

## 3. How can missing or inaccurate data affect data analysis and decision-making?

Missing or inaccurate data can lead to incorrect results and misleading conclusions. This can affect business decisions because decisions based on unreliable data may not accurately represent the actual situation. Therefore, data should be cleaned and checked before performing analysis.
